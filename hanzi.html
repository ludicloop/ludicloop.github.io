Hanzi Defense

Weaponizing the Language

Hanzi Defense is a unique educational tower defense where the visual grammar of the Chinese language is your only weapon. Build ancient pictograms to defend your base, and stack them to craft powerful new words based on the Wuxing (Five Elements) system.

Active Development

The game is currently in active development. We are continually expanding our character dictionary, refining the elemental synergies, and adding new enemy types that challenge both your strategic mind and your linguistic memory.

Play the MVP

A Minimum Viable Product (MVP) is already live. We want your feedback to guide the next phases of development and make the learning loop flawless.
