Privacy Policy

Last updated: Today

1. The Short Version

At Ludic Loop, we respect your privacy. We build pure gameplay loops, not data-harvesting machines. Our web games (like Hanzi Defense) do not collect any personal data. For our mobile games, we only use standard third-party services to show ads or save your progress.

2. Data We Collect

Web Games (Itch.io / Browser): We do not collect, store, or process any personal data. Your game progress is saved locally on your own browser.

Mobile Games (Android / iOS): We do not ask for your name, email, or account creation. However, standard engine analytics (e.g., Godot) or third-party ad networks (like Google AdMob) may collect anonymous usage data (like crash reports or device type) to serve you relevant ads and help us fix bugs.

3. Third-Party Services

Our mobile games may utilize third-party services that have their own Privacy Policies. These may include:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob
  • Unity Analytics / GameAnalytics (if applicable)

4. Children's Privacy

Our educational and puzzle games are designed to be safe for everyone. We do not knowingly collect identifiable personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent and believe your child has provided us with personal info, please contact us so we can delete it immediately.
