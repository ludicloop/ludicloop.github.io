Don't bug me!

Pure Logic, Zero Fluff

Based on classic spatial and logic mechanics, "Don't bug me!" is a puzzle game that balances cuteness with ruthless brain-teasers. The gameplay relies on pure logic, challenging grids, and a cast of quirky, humorous little bugs that just want to be sorted out.

Inclusive Design

We believe puzzles should be for everyone. The game has been designed from the ground up with accessibility in mind, featuring a fully integrated colorblind-friendly mode so no visual cues are ever lost.

Roadmap & Engine

Currently being forged in the Godot Engine, our rollout plan is set in three phases:

  • 01. itch.io Pre-Release: Coming in a few weeks for early feedback and balancing.
  • 02. Google Play Store: Full Android release optimized for mobile devices.
  • 03. Apple App Store: iOS port to bring the bugs to every pocket.
